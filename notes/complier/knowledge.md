You're referring to something from **formal language theory** — probably **Kleene star** and **Kleene closure**.

Let’s make it **clear and short**:

---

### ✅ **Kleene Star** (aka **Kleene Closure**)

* Not “whole star” or “clean star clousore” — the correct term is **Kleene star**.
* Written as: `A*`
* It means: **Zero or more repetitions** of symbols from a set (alphabet or language).

---

### 📘 Example:

If `A = {a}`,
then `A* = {ε, a, aa, aaa, aaaa, ...}`
(ε means empty string)

If `B = {ab, c}`,
then `B* = {ε, ab, c, abab, abc, cab, ababab, ...}`

---

### ✅ Summary:

* **Kleene star (`*`)** = "repeat 0 or more times"
* It’s used in **regular expressions** and **automata theory**
* It's called **Kleene** (pronounced "KLEE-nee") after the mathematician Stephen Kleene

---

If you saw "whole star" or "clean star clousore" — those are likely just **misheard or misspelled** versions of **Kleene star / Kleene closure**.
